The Culture
of Coffee

The rituals, traditions, and craft of coffee form a living heritage integral to the daily lives of millions around the world. At the Coffee Science Centre and the Barista Academy, we celebrate, share, and interpret this rich culture. By bringing together science, emotion, and the art of preparation, we help safeguard the future of the coffee tradition that defines us.

Coffee Science Centre

Explore

In this immersive space, the universe of coffee is revealed through experiences, interactive equipment, and exhibitions. Here, questions find answers, stories unfold, and the Portuguese cultural and scientific heritage in the field of coffee comes to light.

Barista Academy

Explore

At Academia Barista, we share the art and passion of coffee, with a strong emphasis on training. Our team of specialists begins with the espresso to highlight creativity and expertise, showcasing the impressive results that stem from technical innovation.
