About me
I’m a Danish designer and creative director based in Aarhus, working globally. With a background in communication, I approach design beyond aesthetics. Form follows meaning. 
Growing up between cultures and spending much of my career across three continents continues to shape how I think, collaborate and create.
Philosophy
My work is rooted in minimalism because clarity creates stronger experiences. I strip away what distracts so what matters can be felt, not just seen.
