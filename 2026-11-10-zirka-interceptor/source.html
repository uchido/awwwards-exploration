First strikes on Ukraine

The use of Iranian UAV, Shahed-136, began with attacks on Ukraine in October 2022. These were massive kamikaze drones with pre-programmed flight paths that targeted civilian infrastructure.

Geran-2: Russia’s adaptation of Shahed

In recent years, Russia has scaled up mass production for drone upgrades. The warhead can now reach up to 90 kg, the flight range exceeds 1,000 km, and the system can be remotely controlled in real time, functioning similarly to a large FPV drone.

From drone to terror platform

Today, Shahed-type UAVs perform both strike and reconnaissance missions, engage dynamic targets, evade interceptors, and form airborne mesh networks. They are deployed in large-scale swarms, overwhelming air defense system.

Mass attacks capable of disrupting a country

A single large-scale strike can plunge a European country into blackout overnight. Within a week, it can disable critical infrastructure. Within a month, it can severely destabilize an entire state.

Ukrainians are the only ones who can fight back

During the winter of 2025–2026 alone, Russia launched nearly 19,000 Shahed-type strike drones against Ukraine. The country held its ground, primarily due to the operational deployment of interceptor UAV units.
