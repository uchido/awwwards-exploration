Cartier Le Choeur des pierres

An immersive journey into Cartier High Jewellery

  • Design
  • Real-Time 3D

For Cartier, makemepulse created Le Chœur des Pierres, a digital experience designed to bring a new sense of depth to High Jewellery discovery.

See Live

Where every creation begins

In High Jewellery, the stone is never simply a material. It is the starting point of every creation.

Its colour, light and character shape the design of the final piece. This principle informed the entire experience: users choose a gemstone, enter its world, and discover the necklace or ring it inspired.

By placing the gemstone at the centre, the experience offers a more intimate way to understand the singularity of each Cartier creation.

Gallery

At the heart of each journey is the guaché, the hand-painted design drawing that translates the stone’s singularity into a jewellery creation.


On interaction, the finished piece gives way to the guaché beneath it, revealing the invisible structure behind the jewel. A simple gesture shows how inspiration becomes form, and how Cartier's savoir-faire transforms rarity into creation.

The interface stays intentionally restrained, allowing the gemstones, the drawings and the jewels to remain the focus.

Light, motion and precision

The experience was crafted through a careful balance of layered imagery, light, browser-based motion and subtle visual effects.

Every transition creates a sense of depth while preserving the precision of Cartier's product imagery. Motion remains understated, supporting discovery without ever overpowering the pieces themselves.

The result is an immersive journey that feels fluid, sensorial and true to the materiality of the gemstones.

Designed for a global luxury platform

Le Chœur des Pierres was integrated into the Cartier.com ecosystem and adapted for a multilingual rollout across approximately 20 languages.

The technical approach prioritised stability, performance and consistency, ensuring the experience could scale globally while retaining the feeling of a bespoke digital object.

A quiet moment of revelation

Le Chœur des Pierres transforms a product presentation into a more emotional moment of discovery.

By beginning with the gemstone and revealing the craft behind each piece, the experience creates a digital expression of Cartier High Jewellery that is precise, sensorial and intentionally restrained.
