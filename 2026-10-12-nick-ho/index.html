History
2015 — 2020

The Foundation

Nick Ho, a 21-year-old student of International Business at Erasmus University Rotterdam, began his motorsport journey at a young age. Starting with karting, he built racecraft lap by lap.

2021

First Victories

In 2021, Nick became champion in the X30 Senior class of the GK4 Kart Series, showcasing consistency and speed. His first title — super.

2023 — 2024

Proving Speed

Transition to sprint racing in the Porsche Sprint Challenge Benelux. Driving a Porsche Cayman 718 GT4, he dominated his class, winning every race entered.

2025

Going International

Multiple overall podiums and a first race win in Porsche Carrera Cup Benelux — including victory at Assen on Racing Day. A breakthrough moment.

2026

Pole Position

Next step: Porsche Carrera Cup Deutschland — one of the most competitive one-make series in the world. Rookie pole position target.
