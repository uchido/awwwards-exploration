The Foundation

Nick Ho, a 21-year-old student of International Business at Erasmus University Rotterdam, began his motorsport journey at a young age. Starting with karting, he quickly developed a strong passion for racing and competed in various national and international championships, gaining valuable experience and sharpening his racecraft.

First Victories

In 2021, Nick reached a major milestone by becoming the champion in the X30 Senior class of the GK4 Kart Series, showcasing his consistency and speed. The following year, he made his debut in car racing during the Zandvoort 500 endurance race, teaming up with Wouter Boerekamps in the GT4 class. Their strong performance, resulting in a class win, strengthened Nick’s ambition to pursue a professional career in motorsport.

Proving Speed

Nick then transitioned to sprint racing, joining the Porsche Sprint Challenge Benelux. Driving a Porsche Cayman 718 GT4, he dominated his class, winning every race of the season. This success laid the foundation for his step up to the Porsche Carrera Cup Benelux with PG Motorsport in 2024, marking his debut in the Porsche 992 GT3 Cup car, a significant challenge known for its pure, demanding nature.

Going International

In 2025, Nick continued his development within the series, achieving multiple overall podiums and securing his first race win, a breakthrough moment that underlined his progression. That same year, he made a guest appearance in the Porsche Carrera Cup Deutschland with Team GP Elite, where he claimed rookie pole position and finished on the podium in both races, marking an impressive debut that highlighted his potential at an international level.

Pole position

In 2026, Nick aims to take the next step in his career by competing in the Porsche Carrera Cup Deutschland, one of the most competitive and prestigious one-make series in Europe. Alongside this, he plans to take part in the final editon of the Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup race on his home track, Circuit Zandvoort, held during the Formula 1 weekend. To make this international program a reality, Nick is currently looking for new partners to join his journey, brands that share his passion for performance, precision, and ambition.
