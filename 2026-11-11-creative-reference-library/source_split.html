CRL® is a curated library of creative references, built from work that has proven its value through continued use. It brings together sound, film, writing, and material that continue to inform how ideas are developed, tested, and executed in practice, across different stages of the creative process. Spanning multiple disciplines—music, design, film, and art—the library reflects a considered approach to input. It is shaped through selection and return, not volume or trend. Each reference is included for its ability to remain relevant, offering insight into the thinking, process, and standards that underpin meaningful creative work, and how those standards are applied over time.

The library spans a range of formats, including interviews, films, books, and music. From long-form conversations to ambient records, each entry offers a different way into the work—whether through process, perspective, or atmosphere. Together, these references form a resource for ongoing creative development, supporting different ways of thinking, working, and approaching the craft over time.
