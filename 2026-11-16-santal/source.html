Our Approach

seeheartouchfeel

Inspiration defines
direction — engineering secures it.

We translate vision into architecture through a structured development process.

Intent Before Image

Every project begins with a clear architectural direction defined through proportion, hierarchy, and spatial logic. Form must remain coherent under structural and technical evaluation.

Engineering
as Foundation

Structural systems, tolerances, and interdisciplinary coordination are resolved early. Architecture must be buildable, rational, and exact — not merely expressive.

Material Intelligence

Project

Complete skeleton door design

Materials are selected for performance, longevity, tactility, and light interaction. Authenticity is essential. Every surface must justify its presence structurally and atmospherically.

Detail
as Discipline

Junctions, transitions, alignments, and finishing tolerances are refined through multiple development stages. Ambiguity is eliminated before construction begins.

Digital Infrastructure

Nothing is secondary

We believe that the quality of a project is defined as much by the decisions that remain unseen as by those that are immediately visible.

The same principle extends to our own digital infrastructure. SANTAL’s production website is hosted in Switzerland within Flow Swiss’ ALP2 region — an underground data centre built inside a former civil-defence bunker in Lucerne.

Its infrastructure combines physical resilience with responsible energy use: the facility operates on renewable energy, uses water from Lake Lucerne for cooling, and returns recovered server heat to the local district heating network.

For us, this is not an isolated technical decision. It reflects a broader principle: security, longevity, efficiency and responsible use of resources should be considered at every scale.
